CalcStash Privacy Policy

This policy describes the information involved in using CalcStash and how to ask questions about your data.

Information you provide

CalcStash is designed to work with calculations, calculation history, saved values, and optional labels or notes. This information is used to provide the calculator and saved-number features you choose to use. Do not include sensitive personal information unless you are comfortable storing it in the app.

Device permissions and storage

App features may use device storage and iOS permissions. You can review and change available permissions in iOS Settings. Information may also be affected by your device backup settings. The calculator's saved-number and history collections are stored locally on your device, and these features do not require an account. Device backups may include locally stored app information depending on your settings.

Third-party services and sharing

CalcStash may rely on Apple platform services and software libraries to provide app functionality. The available release documentation describes the saved-number and calculation-history features as local. Final App Store privacy disclosures will be checked against the submitted build, including any third-party software behavior.

Retention, deletion, and choices

Information may remain in the app until you remove it, clear applicable app data, or uninstall the app. Copies in device backups may persist under Apple's backup settings. If you need assistance understanding or deleting app data, email support@keviero.com. Do not uninstall the app solely to troubleshoot without considering the possibility of losing locally stored data.

Children's privacy

CalcStash is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app and its data practices. Questions: support@keviero.com.
