EZAlbum Privacy Policy

This policy describes the information involved in using EZAlbum and how to ask questions about your data.

Information you provide

EZAlbum is designed to work with photos, album names, and photo organization choices. This information is used to provide the photo selection and album organization features you choose to use. Do not include sensitive personal information unless you are comfortable storing it in the app.

Device permissions and storage

App features may use device storage and iOS permissions. You can review and change available permissions in iOS Settings. Information may also be affected by your device backup settings. Specific storage, sharing, and synchronization behavior must be consistent with the version of the app you install.

Third-party services and sharing

EZAlbum may rely on Apple platform services and software libraries to provide app functionality. Any third-party data processing and applicable disclosures will be reviewed against the release build before App Store publication. We do not describe a feature as private, encrypted, or free of data collection without verifying its actual behavior.

Retention, deletion, and choices

Information may remain in the app until you remove it, clear applicable app data, or uninstall the app. Copies in device backups may persist under Apple's backup settings. If you need assistance understanding or deleting app data, email support@keviero.com. Do not uninstall the app solely to troubleshoot without considering the possibility of losing locally stored data.

Children's privacy

EZAlbum is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app and its data practices. Questions: support@keviero.com.
