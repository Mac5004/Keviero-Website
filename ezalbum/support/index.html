EZAlbum Support

Need help with EZAlbum? Email support@keviero.com.

Before contacting support

Include the app version, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots can help, but please avoid sending sensitive information.

If your data is missing, avoid uninstalling the app until you have considered whether locally stored information could be lost.

Privacy and data questions

Read the EZAlbum privacy policy or email us with your question.
