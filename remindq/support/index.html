Questions about RemindQ? We're here to help you keep life's important responsibilities on track.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create or edit a reminder?

Open RemindQ, create a reminder, enter its details and save. To make changes, open an existing reminder and use its available editing controls.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Check Settings → Notifications → RemindQ on your iPhone and allow notifications. Also review Focus settings, your reminder's due date, and any advance-alert settings. If alerts still fail, contact support with your iOS and app versions.

How do I manage repeating reminders?

Open the reminder and review the available repeat options. If an expected schedule is missing or behaves unexpectedly, contact support with the schedule you attempted to set.

What if a reminder is not saving or updating?

Close and reopen RemindQ, then check whether the change appears. Please report persistent problems before reinstalling, since reinstalling may affect locally stored app data.

How can I request data deletion or ask a privacy question?

Email support@keviero.com with “RemindQ privacy request” in the subject. We will explain the applicable options for your data.

Contact support

Email support@keviero.com. For technical issues, include your app version, iOS version, the steps that caused the issue, and any relevant screenshots. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal details.
