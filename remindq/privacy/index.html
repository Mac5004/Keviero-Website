RemindQ Privacy Information

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Keviero provides RemindQ, an app for organizing reminders and recurring responsibilities. This page explains the data and device capabilities involved in using the app. For questions about privacy, contact support@keviero.com.

Reminder information

Information you choose to enter in RemindQ may include reminder titles, dates, notes, schedules, categories, and attachments. RemindQ uses this information to display and manage reminders and to support the features you choose to use. Avoid entering sensitive information you do not want stored in your reminders.

Device storage and iCloud

RemindQ uses device storage for app information. iCloud synchronization is optional and off by default. If you enable it, reminder records can sync using Apple's CloudKit service. This includes reminder details and attachment metadata, but not the actual attached photo or document files. Attachment files remain on the device where they were added. Apple's iCloud settings and policies also apply.

Notifications

RemindQ schedules reminder notifications locally through iOS. The inspected notification code does not use a remote push-notification service. You can control notification permission in iOS Settings. Notification content may appear on your device's lock screen depending on your iOS settings.

Photos and documents

RemindQ includes functionality for selecting photos and documents to attach to reminders. Access is initiated through the relevant system picker when you choose to add an attachment. The selected file is copied into local RemindQ app storage. The file itself does not sync through RemindQ's iCloud feature, although its metadata may be included in a synced reminder. Please consider the information in any attachments before adding them.

Third-party services

RemindQ relies on Apple platform services for certain device and iCloud functionality. The app also uses software components provided by Expo and other development libraries. This page does not claim that every library or service is a separate recipient of your personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Reminder information remains subject to the storage and synchronization mechanisms of the app and your device. Removing a reminder in the app may not immediately remove copies retained in device backups or synchronized services. For assistance with deletion or to ask what data is associated with your use of RemindQ, email support@keviero.com. We will explain the applicable options.

Children's privacy

RemindQ is intended as a general-purpose reminder utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes to this information

We may update this page as RemindQ's features and data practices change. The updated date above will reflect material revisions.
